# Sutra brand options

Six directions for a new Sutra mark and eight app icons, replacing the knot-S on gradient tiles. Open `index.html` (one self-contained page: works offline, on a phone, light and dark) to compare them; it starts with a comparison table and the current logo for reference, and ends with a ranked recommendation and a roll-out plan.

| Folder | Idea | Mark | App tiles |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `01-bead` | pearls on a thread | ring bead strung over and under a thread | cream tile, one dyed disc on a thread |
| `02-loom` | warp and weft | 4 by 4 plain weave | dyed tile, a different weave per app |
| `03-stitch` | sew, suture, seam | needle with a running-stitch thread | dyed tile with a dashed stitched hem |
| `04-aphorism` | terse lines strung together | one thread through four ragged lines | kohl tile, paper lines, one dyed line |
| `05-devanagari` | the headline bar and स | shirorekha bar with a reduced स (not traced from a font) | dyed tile, pictogram hanging from a bar |
| `06-spool` | a reel of thread | spool with a loose end | plain dyed tile, large pictogram |

Palette: eight natural textile dyes, one per app (indigo, madder red, turmeric, saffron, pomegranate, lac, henna green, kohl), defined in OKLCH in `build.mjs`; a paper-coloured glyph on every tile reaches at least 3:1 (lowest: turmeric 3.4:1). Everything is flat: no gradients, shadows or bevels.

Each option folder holds `mark.svg` (colour), `mark-mono.svg` (`currentColor`), `mark-reversed.svg` (dark backgrounds), `favicon.svg`, `lockup*.svg` (mark plus a drawn `sutra` wordmark, no font needed) and `app-icons/<app>.svg` (1024 grid, plus `sutra.svg` for the suite icon).

## Rebuild

Everything, including the SVGs and `index.html`, is generated by one script with no dependencies (Node 18 or later):

```sh
node design/brand-options/build.mjs
```

Marks are drawn in a 64-unit box; the app icons use the same drawing scaled by 16 onto the 1024 grid. To change an option edit its section in `build.mjs` (geometry functions, then the `options.push({...})` block with copy, pros and cons). The script also exports its helpers (`options`, `dye`, `contrast`, `weave`, `wordmarkPath`) for experiments.

Notes: the 1024 icons carry rounded corners for preview; export full-bleed squares for iOS and Android (they apply their own mask). The `स` is a geometric abstraction of the first letter of सूत्र, not the exact letter: have a Devanagari reader review it before it ships.
